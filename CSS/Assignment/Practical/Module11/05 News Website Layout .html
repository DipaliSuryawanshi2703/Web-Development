Parliament Budget Session 2026 Today Live Updates: Opposition pushes for removal on Lok Sabha speaker Om Birla, Congress MP Dr Mohammad Jawed moves a resolution

Parliament Budget Session 2026 Live Updates: A motion seeking the removal of Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla was tabled in Parliament on Tuesday. Congress MP Dr Mohammad Jawed moved the resolution in the Lok Sabha, alleging that the Chair has failed to maintain the impartiality required to command the confidence of all sections of the House. During the discussion, TMC MP Mahua Moitra asked Speaker to “go, in the name of God”, saying Birla was “no longer fit to conduct the affairs of the House”. In the resolution, Jawed accused the Speaker of disallowing the Leader of the Opposition and other Opposition leaders from speaking, making unwarranted allegations against women MPs from the Opposition, and suspending Opposition members for an entire session for raising issues of public concern. The motion also alleges that the Speaker did not rebuke ruling party members for making objectionable and derogatory remarks against former Prime Ministers.
